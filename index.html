MC Management

Privacy Open the books

Running a motorcycle club, on your own phone. The roll, the subs, the meetings, the votes, the charters and every report that has to be printed.

What it does

  • Charges every active member his subs in one press, every month
  • Keeps the club account to the cent, and ties it up to the bank statement
  • A statement for every brother, printed or sent to him off the phone
  • Prospects, applications, emergency details and the families
  • Minutes, actions, decisions, and a record of who is turning up
  • Votes: who was for, who was against, and whether it carried
  • The meeting pack, the year end, and a summary for the region
  • National, the regions and the charters — laid out once, sent to every charter

Where it all lives

On the handset, and nowhere else. There is no account to make, no cloud and no subscription. Backups are written out when you press the button and handed to the phone’s own share sheet, so where they go is your choice.

How to use it

A narrated walkthrough runs through every screen, about twelve minutes. It is built into the app under How to use, so it plays with no signal.

For a club with more than one charter

There is a second edition that keeps the books in the club's own database instead of on one phone. Each man signs in as himself and sees what his office allows: the Treasurer changes the money, the Secretary keeps the minutes, a brother sees his own statement, and the regional and national office bearers see their charters add up. Open the books if your club is set up for it.
